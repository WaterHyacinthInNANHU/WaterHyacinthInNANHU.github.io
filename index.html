Selected Research

  1. AXIS-Bench: What Matters in Quality-Aware VLA Pretraining

    Mingxuan Yan, Litian Gong, Dihong Huang, Zhixu Li, Tianyu Zhang, Wenqian Zhang, Hefeifei Jiang, Peihao Li, Jian Zhang, Mengfei Zhao, Yikai Tang, Hai Zhai, Zehao Wang, Ruijian Liang, Yanjia Huang, Lin Shao, Changliu Liu, Kaiyu Hang, Zhiwen Fan, Masayoshi Tomizuka, Jianfei Yang, Jiachen Li

    Under review, 2026

    A controlled benchmark reveals how demonstration quality shapes VLA pretraining.

    We compare weighting, filtering, and conditioning on shared demonstrations with matched training budgets. Across three VLAs, episode-level conditioning improves mean task success, while the best label granularity depends on the method and model. Replayable demonstrations also support real-world validation.

    Paper Project

  2. AXIS: A Growable Community-Driven Data Engine for Scalable Robot Manipulation

    Mengfei Zhao*, Dihong Huang*, Yikai Tang*, Peihao Li*, Mingxuan Yan*, Ruiqi Zhuang*, Yanjia Huang*, Jie Wang, Hai Zhai, Tony Zhou, Rui Zhang, Zhexi Luo, Yuchen Huang, Jianfei Yang, Jiachen Li

    IROS 2026 Workshop on Physical World Models for Scaling Embodied AI

    * Equal contribution.

    A browser-based data engine turns community demonstrations into scalable robot training data.

    Contributors collect simulated robot demonstrations directly in a web browser. AXIS generates and validates tasks, refines trajectories, and augments scenes to grow the dataset over time. Pretraining on AXIS improves policy robustness on LIBERO-Plus.

    Paper Project Dataset

  3. RDD: Retrieval-Based Demonstration Decomposer for Planner Alignment in Long-Horizon Tasks

    Mingxuan Yan, Yuping Wang, Zechun Liu, Jiachen Li

    NeurIPS 2025

    Aligning planner supervision with robot skills improves long-horizon manipulation.

    RDD decomposes long demonstrations by retrieving visually similar sub-tasks from the low-level policy’s training data. These segments provide aligned supervision for the high-level planner, bringing downstream task success close to that obtained with expert segmentation.

    Paper Project Code

  4. UniOcc: A Unified Benchmark for Occupancy Forecasting and Prediction in Autonomous Driving

    Yuping Wang*, Xiangyu Huang*, Xiaokang Sun*, Mingxuan Yan, Shuo Xing, Zhengzhong Tu, Jiachen Li

    ICCV 2025

    * Equal contribution.

    Unified occupancy data connects perception, forecasting, and cooperative driving.

    UniOcc brings real and simulated driving datasets into a common format with occupancy and voxel-level motion labels. Shared evaluation covers current and future occupancy, including shape plausibility and temporal consistency without requiring ground-truth occupancy.

    Paper Project Code

  5. DetVPCC: RoI-based Point Cloud Sequence Compression for 3D Object Detection

    Mingxuan Yan, Ruijie Zhang, Xuedou Xiao, Wei Wang

    arXiv preprint, 2025

    Prioritizing object regions improves point-cloud compression for 3D detection.

    DetVPCC uses a lightweight detector to identify regions likely to contain objects, then assigns them higher coding quality within VPCC. This preserves detection-relevant geometry and improves the bitrate-accuracy trade-off on nuScenes.

    Paper

  1. Task-Oriented Video Compressive Streaming for Real-time Semantic Segmentation

    Xuedou Xiao, Yingying Zuo, Mingxuan Yan, Wei Wang, Jianhua He, Qian Zhang

    IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing, 2024

    Model-guided video compression preserves the details needed for semantic segmentation.

    STAC uses network gradients to identify regions where compression most affects segmentation accuracy. It combines spatial and temporal compression with a learned bandwidth-aware controller, reducing transmission cost while adapting to changing video content and network conditions.

    Paper

  2. From Ember to Blaze: Swift Interactive Video Adaptation via Meta-Reinforcement Learning

    Xuedou Xiao*, Mingxuan Yan*, Yingying Zuo, Boxi Liu, Paul Ruan, Yang Cao, Wei Wang

    IEEE INFOCOM 2023

    * Equal contribution.

    Meta-learning enables rapid bitrate adaptation as network conditions change.

    Fiammetta learns an adaptable streaming policy offline and updates it online with a few gradient steps. Evaluated on real-world WeChat for Business network traces, it increases video bitrate without increasing the stalling rate.

    Paper
