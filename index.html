About

My research focuses on scalable robot learning. I study how to collect and learn from diverse demonstrations, use simulation as a scalable source of experience for real-world robots, and organize long demonstrations into training data for robot planning.

Research Interests

  • Scalable robot learning: community-driven data (AXIS) and quality-aware pretraining (AXIS-Bench).
  • Sim-to-real transfer: workspace-aligned simulation pretraining (AXIS-Bench, AXIS).
  • Long-horizon manipulation: skill-aligned demonstration segmentation (RDD).

    AXIS-Bench: What Matters in Quality-Aware VLA Pretraining

    Mingxuan Yan, Litian Gong, Dihong Huang, Zhixu Li, Tianyu Zhang, Wenqian Zhang, Hefeifei Jiang, Peihao Li, Jian Zhang, Mengfei Zhao, Yikai Tang, Hai Zhai, Zehao Wang, Ruijian Liang, Yanjia Huang, Lin Shao, Changliu Liu, Kaiyu Hang, Zhiwen Fan, Masayoshi Tomizuka, Jianfei Yang, Jiachen Li

    Under review, 2026

    Built on AXIS, this benchmark studies how to use demonstration quality in VLA pretraining through controlled experiments on replayable data with simulator-grounded quality labels. Episode-level conditioning improves mean success across all three tested models. We also provide a real-world BC baseline pretrained only on workspace-aligned simulation data.

  2. AXIS: A Growable Community-Driven Data Engine for Scalable Robot Manipulation

    Mengfei Zhao*, Dihong Huang*, Yikai Tang*, Peihao Li*, Mingxuan Yan*, Ruiqi Zhuang*, Yanjia Huang*, Jie Wang, Hai Zhai, Tony Zhou, Rui Zhang, Zhexi Luo, Yuchen Huang, Jianfei Yang, Jiachen Li

    * Equal contribution

    IROS 2026 Workshop

  3. RDD: Retrieval-Based Demonstration Decomposer for Planner Alignment in Long-Horizon Tasks

    Mingxuan Yan, Yuping Wang, Zechun Liu, Jiachen Li

    NeurIPS 2025

    RDD turns long demonstrations into planner training segments matched to a robot’s learned skills. It retrieves similar examples from the robot policy’s training data to choose the segment boundaries.

    Paper Project Code
